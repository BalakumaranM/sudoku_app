Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 17, 2024

Mini Sudoku ("App", "we", "us", or "our") is a Sudoku puzzle game built with Flutter. We are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to provide and improve your app experience:

  • Device Information: Model, OS version, and Advertising ID (if permitted by your device settings).
  • Usage Data: Anonymous gameplay statistics (levels completed, hints used) for analytics and difficulty tuning.
  • Purchase History: Subscription status and receipt validation for Premium features processed via Google Play / App Store.

2. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted third-party services that may collect data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase: Used for crash reporting (Crashlytics) and anonymous performance analytics.
  • Google AdMob: Serves advertisements. AdMob may use advertising identifiers to deliver relevant ads.
  • Google Play Services: Manages In-App Purchases and subscription status securely.

3. Advertising Policy

We use Google AdMob to display ads in the non-premium version of the application:

  • Rewarded Ads: You voluntarily watch these ads to earn optional game rewards (such as extra hints or second chances).
  • Interstitial Ads: Displayed occasionally between game rounds.
  • You can customize or reset your Ad Identifier via your device settings at any time.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

We offer an optional "Premium" subscription to remove advertisements and access bonus features.

  • Payments are handled directly and securely by Google Play Services or Apple App Store. We do not collect or store your payment card details.
  • Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours prior to the current billing period end.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your individual puzzle progress and local high scores are stored locally on your device. Anonymous performance analytics are stored on secure Google Cloud servers.

6. Your Data Rights

  • Opt-Out: You may opt-out of personalized advertising through your mobile OS privacy settings.
  • Data Erasure: Uninstalling the app removes locally stored game data. For cloud analytics inquiry, please contact support.

7. Children's Privacy

Mini Sudoku is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official app store support page or email support.
